How to make a travel itinerary on iPhone, one day at a time

Most trips start the same way: the flight is in your inbox, the hotel is in a chat, and the tickets are in screenshots. A useful travel itinerary is not the longest one; it is the one that tells you what happens today the moment you open it. Travel itinerary template: what each day needs

Whether you use paper, notes or an app, each day of a good itinerary answers the same questions:

FieldWhat to writeExample
TimeDepartures, arrivals, check-in and activity start times9:30 leave for the airport
FlightFlight number, departure and arrival times, terminalOutbound flight and boarding time
HotelHotel name, address, check-in and check-outNights 1–3
ActivitiesSights, restaurants, reservation slotsAfternoon museum, dinner booking
TicketsReference numbers for tickets and bookingsTrain or event ticket reference
Notes and remindersThings to do in advanceReminder the night before to pack passports

Build your itinerary in TripBee Lite

  1. Create your trip. TripBee Lite is designed around one active trip, so the plan that matters now is the first thing you see.
  2. Shape each day on a timeline. Lay out flights, hotel stays and activities on a clear day-by-day timeline.
  3. Keep flight and hotel details together. No more digging through email and chats.
  4. Add notes, reminders and ticket references. Keep them where you will look for them on the day.
  5. Edit when plans change. Update the trip whenever your plans move, or replace it with your next one.

Packing list tips

Group your packing list by documents and tickets, clothes, toiletries, electronics and medicine, and check items off the night before. TripBee Lite's smart packing list is part of Premium, a one-time in-app purchase; before you unlock it, you can keep packing notes or reminders on the relevant day.
