What is TripBee Lite?

TripBee Lite is an iPhone trip planner that puts each day of your vacation on one timeline: morning flight, transfers, sights, hotel check-in and dinner, in order, with the gaps between stops worked out for you. Flight, hotel and ticket details and notes stay with the day they belong to, and before you leave there is a countdown and the local weather. You can also see your next stop on Apple Watch.

Who is TripBee Lite for?

Travellers who plan their own vacation day by day, friends or families who want to plan one trip together, and anyone who wants flight, hotel and ticket details sorted by the day they belong to. Apple Watch users can check the next stop from the wrist.

How does TripBee Lite charge?

Free download with an in-app purchase. The free app saves one journey at a time, which you can edit or replace whenever plans change. iCloud sync is included for everyone with no purchase: journeys stay up to date on all your devices, and you can invite friends to a trip and edit it together. A one-time Premium purchase unlocks unlimited saved journeys, polished trip exports with photos and tickets, complete backup and restore, and the smart packing list, for the lifetime of the app. There is no subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is TripBee Lite different from other trip planners?

Based on what the listing describes: each day is one ordered timeline with the gaps between stops calculated; bookings and notes sit on the day they belong to; shared editing with friends through iCloud is free; and the next stop shows on Apple Watch. No account is required, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.
